Portfolio · Round 01 · Palette + Type

Five directions, all blue-led, none safe.

Pulling from your refs — Radiohead's prismatic confidence, the surreal pool-tile cyan from the cowboy shot, Madeon & Discovery's dusk gradients, Avicii's ink-and-paper restraint, and a wildcard cyanotype zine — the goal is to find the one tone of voice your work will live inside.

Each tile is a record sleeve: a name lockup as the "cover," the palette as a strip below, and a type pairing applied to a display + body sample. Pan/zoom the canvas. Open any one fullscreen with the expand button (top-right of each card).
